## Platform

## Users

Two audiences, equally important:

- **Recruiters and HR screeners** at Indonesian insurers, regulators/public institutions and data or risk analytics employers. They arrive from the link on the CV or LinkedIn while screening applicants, often on a phone, and decide within a minute or two whether to shortlist.
- **Hiring managers and senior actuaries** who open the same page to judge technical depth: they read the thesis and research results closely and check that claims hold up.

- Static HTML/CSS/JS only; no build step, no framework.
- English only (owner decided against an EN/ID toggle).
- The page must work on phones, in light and dark mode.
- A public copy of the CV without the phone number is published; the phone number must not appear on the site.
- Link preview card (`og-card.png`) shows name, role, GPA and PAI exams; it must be kept in sync with the page when facts change.
- No replica dashboards from internships (owner decision: confidentiality and no invented numbers).
- Monte Carlo and option-spread projects are team work (5 authors each) and must stay labelled as such. The Monte Carlo paper is a draft, not published.
- Thesis code publication is on hold (IPB holds thesis copyright).

## Product Principles

1. Every claim on the page traces to the CV or a project document; nothing is invented or inflated.
2. Recruiter first screen: role, current job, GPA, PAI exams and CV download are visible without scrolling.
3. Depth on demand: technical readers can open full results, data tables and source papers.
4. Credit is explicit: team work is labelled, sources and licenses are recorded.
5. Personal data stays minimal: email and LinkedIn only.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable on phones and in both colour schemes; charts carry a text/table alternative; keyboard-operable project browser.
